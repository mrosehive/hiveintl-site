01 — Digital Forensics & Case Management

Forensically sound institutions, built from the ground up.

Labs and investigators fail not for lack of evidence, but for lack of forensically sound process. Tools dropped into an institutional vacuum don't produce court-admissible results.

Hive is a recognized leader in digital forensics capacity building for law enforcement, prosecutorial, and judicial institutions. Our Digital Forensics Investigative Advisors (DFIAs) work directly alongside lab personnel and investigators, building forensically sound processes from the ground up — scalable from mentorship of a single examiner to sustained advisory across a multi-agency program.

Hive deploys a customized case-management system tailored to both field investigators and digital forensics labs, so evidence and casework flow seamlessly between the two. And with the recent addition of senior DFIAs, Hive increasingly supports the investigations themselves — the cases that turn on digital forensic evidence — not just the institutions that produce it.

In practice, that means accelerating violent-crime and gang investigations, reconstructing communications and GPS metadata for homicide cases, disrupting migrant-smuggling networks, and increasingly supporting investigations into cryptocurrency-enabled illicit finance and encrypted communications across multiple jurisdictions.

Services

Embedded DFIA mentorship · SOPs benchmarked to SWGDE, NIST & ISO/IEC 17025 · custom open-source indexed data repositories · digital, tablet-based evidence intake · international lab accreditation pathways · mobile, vehicle & maritime device forensics · communications & GPS-metadata reconstruction · cryptocurrency & encrypted-communications investigations · integrated field-and-lab case management

02 — Strategic & Transnational Organized Crime Investigations

Mentorship through active casework — and execution in the field.

High-profile and multi-jurisdictional cases stall without seasoned investigative judgment and access to specialized capabilities.

Hive deploys veteran law enforcement advisors — including Senior Investigative Advisors (SIAs) — who embed directly with partner agencies on their most significant cases. We work inside formal task forces and alongside individual agencies alike; the structure matters less than the result. Our model is mentorship through active casework across the full investigative lifecycle, from surveillance and evidence collection through arrest, prosecution, and conviction. When a case is ready, we help operationalize it in the field — as in a coordinated dual-site operation in St. Kitts & Nevis in 2025.

When a case needs a capability an agency cannot reach on its own, Hive can help connect the program to specialized U.S. federal resources — cellular-location analysis, vehicle and digital forensic exploitation, electronic-storage detection canines, and more. These capabilities are requested and coordinated case by case; they are owned by the agencies that provide them, and availability rests with those agencies. What Hive brings is the relationships, the judgment to know which capability fits, and the discipline to put it to use correctly.

Services

Embedded Senior Investigative Advisors · operational support on live cases · advisory support to agencies within or outside a task force · cross-border intelligence sharing · case-by-case coordination of specialized federal capabilities (cellular-location analysis, vehicle & digital forensics, detection canines) · MOU facilitation · task force standup & SOP development

03 — Border Security & Targeting

From reactive enforcement to intelligence-led targeting.

Too many agencies remain stuck in reactive enforcement when the data exists to drive informed, predictive targeting.

Hive delivers expert advisory services to customs and border security agencies, operationalizing the tools, targeting frameworks, and data governance structures that produce real interdiction results. Our Customs Enforcement Advisors (CEAs) embed with regional targeting centers and front-line agencies.

Increasingly, this is forward defense for the United States itself. By helping regional partners detect narcotics traffickers, fraudulent travelers, illicit cargo, and irregular-migration networks before they reach U.S. shores, Hive supports a forward detection architecture aligned with U.S. border-security priorities — coordinated with partners that include CBP, ICE, EUROPOL, and INTERPOL. That coordination has contributed to outcomes such as the discovery of a transnational stolen-vehicle network spanning more than 300 vehicles across multiple Caribbean states.

Services

Embedded Customs Enforcement Advisors · advanced passenger & cargo data systems (APIS/ACIS, ATS-G, Windward) · PNR policy & model-legislation development · airport & seaport interdiction methodology · targeting-rule development · CARICOM-IMPACS / JRCC / Regional Intelligence Fusion Centre coordination · interagency coordination with CBP, ICE, EUROPOL & INTERPOL · risk profiling

04 — Monitoring, Evaluation & Data Visualization

Beyond compliance reporting.

M&E that is mere compliance reporting doesn't drive program improvement. Hive's does.

Hive designs and implements monitoring and evaluation frameworks that drive genuine program improvement, not just compliance reporting. Our practitioners are power users of DevResults — the cloud platform the U.S. Department of State uses to track program indicators, targets, and results — and led one of its first field rollouts in the Eastern Caribbean. That rollout went well enough that Hive was subsequently asked to train other implementing partners on the platform, and Hive and INL Bridgetown were jointly nominated for a Department of State Data for Diplomacy Award for the work.

Around that core we layer change maps, disaggregated indicator frameworks, Kirkpatrick Level 2/3 evaluation, and qualitative synthesis in NVivo — and produce Power BI and Tableau visualizations that give leadership and field teams real-time visibility into how a program is actually performing.

The discipline is measurable. Across three Eastern Caribbean programs and eight reporting periods, Hive's reporting captured 535 evidence lines — 40% of them carrying a direct outcome or impact signal, a portfolio steadily moving beyond activity reporting toward documented results.

Services

DevResults framework design & rollout · disaggregated indicator frameworks · Kirkpatrick Level 2/3 evaluation · NVivo qualitative synthesis · Power BI & Tableau dashboards · quarterly data reports · live program data portals
