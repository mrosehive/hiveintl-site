Proven in the Field

Outcomes, not activity reports.

Digital Forensics

Hive stood up a regional digital forensics laboratory from the ground up and keeps its advisors embedded inside it, working cases alongside the lab's examiners years later. Caseloads grew more than 500%, the lab's procedures became rigorous enough to be cited in court as proof of its credibility, and Hive coordinated the first expert cellular-analysis testimony of its kind in the region — turning phone-location evidence into a courtroom result.

Border Security

Hive's border intelligence program helps partner agencies build risk profiles and intelligence-driven targeting rules — the analytics that flag a suspect shipment or traveler before it moves. It's forward defense: intercepting threats abroad before they reach U.S. shores. That work has surfaced major trafficking attempts, disrupted an irregular migration route, and earned a U.S. Department of State Data for Diplomacy Award nomination.

Institutional Strengthening

Strong institutions outlast any single program. Hive built an international law enforcement alumni network now spanning 70+ countries that keeps professionals connected long after training ends, and introduced evaluation methods rigorous enough that U.S. government leadership adopted them — with a federal oversight commission recommending Hive's approach as a model for the wider region.

"Hive International's staff and advisors have developed key mentoring and advisory relationships and trust across the region leading to meaningful, transformative, and legacy-building INL engagements."
— Contracting Officer's Representative Assessment, INL Bridgetown, U.S. Department of State
