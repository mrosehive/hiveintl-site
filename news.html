Aug 29, 2025 Program Milestone

Modernizing Guatemala's Forensic Computing Laboratory with INACIF

PADF consortium with Hive · INACIF Guatemala

Through a Pan American Development Foundation–led consortium with Hive International, Guatemala's National Institute of Forensic Sciences (INACIF) is modernizing its Computer Forensics Laboratory — including next-generation equipment that doubles analytical capacity and personnel training built for long-term sustainability. The work strengthens Guatemala's justice sector against high-impact transnational crime, from narcotrafficking and money laundering to cybercrime, human trafficking, and corruption.

Jul 22, 2025 Operation

Hive Supports Eastern Caribbean Operation Dismantling Migrant-Smuggling Pipeline

St. Kitts & Nevis · U.S. Dept. of State / INL

Working as an INL implementer alongside regional partners, Hive International supported a recent operation in St. Kitts and Nevis that led to the apprehension of 26 smugglers and dismantled a major migrant-smuggling pipeline directed toward the United States. Hive's embedded strategic investigations advisor and subject-matter experts worked side-by-side with Caribbean law enforcement — sharing methodologies, tools, and training — to stop threats before they reach American shores while building durable regional capacity.

Apr 2023 Recognition

Finalist for the State Department Data for Diplomacy Award

U.S. Department of State · INL Bridgetown & Eastern Caribbean

Hive and INL Bridgetown were named finalists for the U.S. Department of State's Data for Diplomacy Award, recognizing innovative use of monitoring data to sharpen border security across the Eastern Caribbean. The cited innovation was a Hive original — field-friendly data-collection templates frontline operators could complete without friction, translated into manager-ready tables that revealed exactly where law-enforcement follow-up on passenger-data alerts was and was not occurring.
